# Independent reviews: interactive in-situ spectral fits

Two fresh-context reviewers checked the generated HTML, plotting source, and stage descriptions. One assessed reader comprehension and terminology; the other checked scientific correctness and real FITS values for exposure 16819, fiber 489.

The reports consistently separate the last fitted trial, its source continuum, the actual final SKY vector, and resulting sky-subtracted SFrame FLUX. Residual normalization matches native observed IVAR. SKY component sums and observed-minus-SKY identities are asserted during generation. Shared full-range axes, closest-point hover, disabled spikes, and continuously visible content were confirmed.

Applied feedback: expand LSF, ML and DRP, and define CFrame and SFrame in the introductory prose.

Reviewers did not independently assess browser rendering or establish LSF recovery accuracy.
